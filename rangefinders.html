How to choose a rangefinder

Laser vs. GPS

Laser rangefinders give you an exact distance to whatever you point at, which is more precise but requires a clear line of sight. GPS units give distances to preset points on a hole even when the flag isn't visible, trading precision for convenience.

Slope adjustment

Slope-adjusted distances factor in elevation change, which is useful for casual rounds but is against the rules in most sanctioned tournaments — look for a model with a slope on/off switch if you play both.

Magnification and stabilization

Higher magnification and image stabilization make it easier to lock onto a flag at longer distances, especially in windy conditions where your hands may not be perfectly steady.
