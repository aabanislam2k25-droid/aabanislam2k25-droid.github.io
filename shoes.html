How to choose golf shoes

Spiked vs. spikeless

Spiked shoes generally offer more traction on wet or hilly courses, at the cost of comfort walking to and from the car. Spikeless shoes trade a little grip for all-day comfort and the ability to wear them off the course too.

Waterproofing

If you play in wet climates or early morning dew, waterproofing matters more than almost any other feature — a soaked shoe by hole 6 will ruin the rest of your round.

Fit and width

Golf shoes take more lateral stress than running shoes because of the swing motion, so a snug, secure fit through the midfoot matters more than it does in everyday sneakers.
