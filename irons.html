How to choose an iron set

Forged vs. cast

Forged irons generally feel softer at impact and suit players who want more feedback and workability. Cast irons are usually more forgiving and easier to hit consistently, which is why most game-improvement sets are cast.

Shaft material

Steel shafts offer more control and are the standard for players who prioritize accuracy. Graphite shafts are lighter and generate more clubhead speed, which helps players with slower swings get the ball airborne.

Set makeup

Most sets run 4-PW or 5-PW. If you already carry strong wedges, look for a set that starts higher (6 or 7 iron) and leave the long-iron distance gaps to a hybrid instead.
