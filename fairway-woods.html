How to choose a fairway wood or hybrid

Fairway wood vs. hybrid

Fairway woods generally launch the ball lower and roll out further, which suits players who want to run the ball up onto the green. Hybrids launch higher and stop faster, making them easier to control from rough or tight lies.

Filling the distance gap

Most players benefit from a 3-wood off the tee and a hybrid to replace their longest, hardest-to-hit irons — usually the 3 and 4 iron.

Loft selection

A higher-lofted fairway wood (16-18°) is easier to launch from the fairway than a low-lofted 3-wood, which mainly performs off a tee.
