How to choose a golf ball

Compression

Lower-compression balls suit slower swing speeds because they deform more easily at impact, converting more of your swing into ball speed. Faster swingers get more benefit from higher-compression balls.

Spin

Higher-spin balls stop faster on the green but can also amplify slices and hooks off the tee. Lower-spin balls fly straighter off the driver but are harder to control around the green.

Construction

2-piece balls are simpler, more durable, and generally cheaper — a good fit for higher handicappers. 3-piece (and up) balls add layers that improve short-game control, which better players value more than the added cost.
