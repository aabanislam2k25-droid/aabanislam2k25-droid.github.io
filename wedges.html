How to choose a wedge

Bounce

Higher bounce helps prevent the club digging into soft turf or sand, which suits steeper swings and softer conditions. Lower bounce suits shallower swings and firmer turf, where you need the leading edge closer to the ground.

Loft gaps

Keep roughly 4-6 degrees between wedges so you have a full-swing distance for every yardage, rather than large gaps that force awkward half-swings.

Grooves

Sharper, deeper grooves generate more spin but wear down faster. If you play often, expect to replace wedges every 1-2 seasons to keep spin consistent.
